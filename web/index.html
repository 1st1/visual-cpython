About CPython X-Ray

  • Real CPython, in your browser. A WebAssembly build runs Python locally in a worker.
  • Compilation made visible. Explore source, tokens, the syntax tree, and bytecode side by side.
  • Live opcode stepping. Pause the interpreter between instructions, or revisit recent execution history.
  • A navigable 3D scene. Three.js renders the program, with Graphviz helping lay out its control flow.
  • Inspect the actual runtime. See frames, locals, the operand stack, and standard output and errors.
